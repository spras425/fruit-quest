🍌 Fruit Quest! + AI Brain 🌿

Turn: 0

🧠 How to Play

You’re exploring a jungle to find fruit trees. Click tiles to discover fruit trees and wait for them to ripen!

  • Green: Not ready
  • Yellow: Almost ripe
  • Red: Ripe and tasty! 🍓

Press "Next Turn" to pass time and ripen fruit.

🤖 What Does the AI Do?

The AI pet remembers which tiles had fruit and learns to choose the ones with the highest reward!

  • It explores randomly at first
  • It gets points when it finds red fruit
  • It learns over time where the best fruit is!
